Net-Net Finder

Graham-style deep value screener

Net current asset value (NCAV) estimates what a company would be worth if it stopped operating today and turned every current asset into cash to pay off every liability. A stock trading below its NCAV/share is arguably backed by more liquid value than its market price reflects — Graham's classic "cigar butt" opportunity.
