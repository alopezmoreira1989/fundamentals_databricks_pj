{% if level == "relaxed" %} NCAV = Total Current Assets (100%) − Total Liabilities. The textbook formula — assumes every dollar of inventory and every dollar of receivables is fully recoverable. {% elif level == "moderate" %} NCAV (Moderate) = Cash + ST Investments (100%) + Receivables (75%) + Inventory (50%) + Other (0%) − Total Liabilities. Common haircuts among net-net investors, not a literal Graham quote. {% else %} NCAV (Strict) = Cash (100%) + ST Investments (90%) + Receivables (50%) + Inventory (33%) + Other (0%) − Total Liabilities. Forced-liquidation value — the toughest filter, fewest false positives. {% endif %} These haircut percentages are a practitioner convention, not an "official" formula — Graham himself varied them across editions of his own writing.
