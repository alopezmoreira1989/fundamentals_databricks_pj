How this forecast is built

Years 1–10 come from a machine-learning model (LightGBM quantile regression) trained cross-sectionally on every company we cover, predicting five outcomes per year — Bear, Low Bear, Crab (the median case), Low Bull, and Bull — rather than a single number. Years 11–20 aren't modeled directly: each scenario's growth rate decays toward this company's own long-run DCF assumption (its terminal growth rate, floored at 2% so no scenario implicitly grows slower than inflation), front-loaded so most of the deceleration happens in the first few years after FY+10. The Forward P/E and FCF Yield table below discounts each year's forecast back to today's dollars at that scenario's own discount rate (WACC). This is a statistical projection, not investment advice — treat the spread between scenarios as the real signal, not any single line.
