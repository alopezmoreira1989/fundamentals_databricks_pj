About

A value-investing screener

This screener is built around the value-investing tradition of Benjamin Graham, Warren Buffett, and Peter Lynch: buy businesses, not tickers, and only with a margin of safety — a price meaningfully below a conservative estimate of intrinsic value, so the estimate can be wrong and the investment still hold up.

The metrics on this site favor balance-sheet discipline over growth narratives — debt levels, liquidity, and the durability of returns on capital — because a company that survives a downturn is worth more than one that merely grows fast through one. Quality is treated as a filter, not a story: profitability, consistency, and financial strength are used to screen candidates in or out before any valuation judgment is made.

Every figure shown is derived mechanically from SEC-filed financials and market prices, following the same formulas for every company. Nothing here is opinion, narrative, or a house view on any individual stock.
